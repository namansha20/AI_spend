## FAQ
1. **Do I need to sign up first?**  
   No. You get full audit results first; email is optional for report capture.
2. **How accurate are the savings estimates?**  
   Estimates use current public pricing and rule-based assumptions; each recommendation includes reasoning.
3. **Will my company details be public in shared links?**  
   No. Public report URLs remove personal/company identifying fields.
4. **What if my savings are small?**  
   The tool says so directly. It won’t invent savings when your setup is already efficient.
5. **What is Credex’s role?**  
   For larger savings opportunities, Credex can help secure discounted AI credits.
