# User interviews

## Interview 1
**Name/role/stage:** A.K., CTO, 18-person B2B SaaS startup

**Quotes:**
- “We have ChatGPT Team and Claude Team because nobody wants to lose productivity, but we never reevaluated the overlap.”
- “Finance just sees one line item called ‘AI tools’ and asks me once a month if it can come down.”
- “If your tool gives me one screenshot I can paste into Slack with savings math, I’ll use it.”

**Most surprising thing:** They preferred a fast estimate over perfect precision, as long as assumptions are explicit.

**What changed in design:** Added clear per-tool reason sentences and screenshot-friendly result layout.

## Interview 2
**Name/role/stage:** M.R., solo founder, pre-seed devtools project

**Quotes:**
- “I spend less than $100/month; don’t upsell me. Just tell me if I’m fine.”
- “Email gate before result is an instant close tab.”
- “I’d share a link if it doesn’t expose my company.”

**Most surprising thing:** Low-spend users still want periodic optimization alerts even when no immediate savings exist.

**What changed in design:** Added honest low-savings messaging + “notify me” style lead capture.

## Interview 3
**Name/role/stage:** S.P., Engineering Manager, Series B fintech (70 employees)

**Quotes:**
- “Our biggest leak is seats that stick around after org changes.”
- “If you suggest alternatives, explain capability parity or people won’t trust it.”
- “For bigger savings I’d absolutely take a vendor intro if it’s concrete.”

**Most surprising thing:** Seat hygiene was a stronger pain point than model-level API optimization for their stage.

**What changed in design:** Added team-size-aware recommendations and a stronger high-savings CTA to Credex consultation.
